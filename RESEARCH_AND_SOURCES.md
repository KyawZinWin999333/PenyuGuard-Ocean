# Research, sourcing and factual boundaries

**Accessed:** 28 October 2026. Claims below are general contextual research; the product does not include real conservation statistics or official integration.

1. **Perhentian Turtle Project** — https://www.perhentianturtleproject.org/ and https://www.perhentianturtleproject.org/about-us. The project describes conservation activities including population surveys, beach patrols, photo identification and stranding responses in the Perhentian Islands. **PenyuGuard is not affiliated with, approved by or integrated with this project.**
2. **Department of Fisheries Malaysia, marine park and turtle-centre management** — https://www.dof.gov.my/en/services/marine-park-resource-management/marine-park-management/ . The Department describes turtle conservation centres and public conservation actions including not disturbing turtles, reducing litter and contacting authorities for stranded turtles.
3. **Department of Fisheries Malaysia, Save Our Turtles** — https://www.dof.gov.my/en/save-our-turtles/ . Provides official guidance to visitors about avoiding disturbance of turtles in Malaysian waters.
4. **Convention on the Conservation of Migratory Species, photo-ID study in Perhentian Islands Marine Park** — https://www.cms.int/publication/using-photographic-identification-monitor-sea-turtle-populations-perhentian-islands . Research illustrates existing photo-identification and observation-based approaches. This is NOT an invented or uniquely new PenyuGuard idea.

## What this prototype does NOT claim

No fictional case corresponds to a real report, population measurement, actual rescue, verified conservation benefit, approved dispatch, wildlife medical diagnosis or official partner workflow. The labels such as "verified" describe **simulated reviewer decisions**, not evidence of true wildlife incidents.

## Visual sources

The Premium Edition uses **real wildlife, reef and island photographs from Wikimedia Commons** and intentionally labels them as illustrative scenery, **not** photographic evidence associated with fictional turtle cases. See `IMAGE_CREDITS.md` for full image sources, licenses and credits. Network access is required for the real photos. If unavailable, an original SVG illustration appears instead.

The custom `assets/ocean-turtle.svg`, map diagram, and icons are original vector graphics.
