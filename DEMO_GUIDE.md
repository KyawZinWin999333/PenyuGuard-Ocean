**Short closing pitch:** "PenyuGuard doesn't claim to rescue turtles on its own. It makes every concern easier to document, every decision easier to trace, and every follow-up easier to coordinate."

## Strong judge answers

**Is this an official reporting app?** No. It is an academic demo with no real emergency service, partner integration or real-world cases.

**Is it AI-powered?** Its current assistant is transparent rule-based support, **not** a trained wildlife health classifier. The innovation is trustworthy case coordination.

**What happens to the data?** All fictional records persist in the same browser's local storage. Production would require authenticated users, a secure server and agreements with approved responders.

**What is different?** The focus is incident-to-outcome workflow integrity: standardized observation, verification rationale, task assignment, follow-up and export in one Rescue Passport. Existing organizations already do turtle monitoring and photo-ID; this prototype is complementary.

**Can it help real conservation right away?** Not without conservation stakeholder research, protocols, official authorization, secure multi-user storage, wildlife-location protections and field validation.
